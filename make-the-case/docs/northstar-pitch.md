# Prepare weekly client updates

FICTIONAL EXAMPLE — all people, evidence, and estimates are illustrative.

Prepared 2026-10-27 · Northstar Client Services

## The decision requested
Authorize four weeks of discovery and a bounded test, subject to tool/data review, with two coordinators and one account lead. Confirm the owner, effort, and budget before starting.

## Why this matters
Company goal: Make weekly client updates accurate and timely without adding overtime.

Problem: Coordinators reconcile notes from three sources for each update. Missing commitments create rework and delay customer communication.

Intended outcome: Reduce net preparation time while maintaining accurate, complete client updates.

## What changes
Today: Collect account notes, compare open issues, draft the update, and check every commitment before an account lead sends it.

Proposed: An approved AI tool prepares a draft from permitted sources. The coordinator checks facts and omissions. The account lead approves and sends the update.

Outside this scope: No new customer commitments, direct sends, CRM write access, or staffing changes in this test.

## People and ownership
Proposed business owner: Account operations lead — proposed, not yet accepted

Collaborators: Account lead for content review; IT/security for access; operations analyst for timing; L&D for a short review checklist.

Affected audiences: Coordinators change preparation and checking. Account leads retain approval. Customers experience the final update. IT/support handle access and exceptions.

Learning and support: A checked example, a practice draft, a fact-check checklist, and a named escalation route for missing or conflicting commitments.
