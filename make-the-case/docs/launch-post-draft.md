# Automation Thursday launch post

Yesterday, Dana’s answer was “a little.”

She had been told to use AI. She had ideas. She needed a way to turn what she knew about the work into a request someone could evaluate.

This week’s Automation Thursday is **Make the Case**: a reusable tool that runs in your own Google Sheet.

Keep your ideas on a board. Pick one. Work through it in a conversation. Build a proposal that shows:

→ What would improve—and for whom.
→ What you know, what you’re estimating, and what is still unknown.
→ Who needs to be involved and what needs approval.
→ The next step you’re asking for.

You do not have to build the solution yourself. Understanding the work, gathering evidence, and bringing the right people together count too.

The walkthrough and GitHub kit are in today’s issue. It’s an early release; I’m putting it out through Automation Thursday to learn what helps and where people get stuck.

What’s one idea you’ve been sitting on—and what would help you bring it forward?
